Exoplanet Atlas

Each dot is a paper. Height is time, with the oldest at the bottom. Papers in the same subfield form a column, and subfields split into smaller subfields inside it. Larger dots are cited more.

Demo data: these dots are synthetic placeholders, not real papers. Add data/papers.json to replace them.

Drag to rotate. Scroll to zoom. Right-drag or two fingers to pan. Click a subfield name to look inside it (Back or Esc goes up), or a paper for details.
